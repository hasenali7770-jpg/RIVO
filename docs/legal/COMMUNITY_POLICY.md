# RIVO — Community and reporting policy (draft)

**Draft for legal review.** Shown before a user's first road report.

RIVO's traffic information is only as good as what drivers tell each other. This
page covers both kinds of report: telling other drivers about the road, and
telling RIVO about a listing.

## Reporting a road condition

You can report an accident, heavy traffic, a closed road, road works, a flooded
road, a pothole or another hazard.

**Report only what you have actually seen, where you actually saw it.**

### How reports become visible

A report from an account with no history is **held for review** and is not shown
to other drivers until a moderator publishes it. An account whose earlier
reports were confirmed by other drivers publishes immediately — up to a limit,
so that no single account can close a road on its own say-so.

Reports expire on their own: a traffic jam has a short life, road works a longer
one. Other drivers can confirm a report, which extends it, or dismiss it, which
removes it.

### Do not

- Report a road closed or blocked when it is not, for any reason.
- Report a hazard to keep traffic away from a route you want clear.
- Report the same thing repeatedly to make it look more serious.
- Report anything about a person. Road conditions only — never a description of
  a driver, a vehicle, an accident victim or a police checkpoint's occupants.

An account whose reports are repeatedly dismissed or removed stops being
published. An account deliberately submitting false reports is suspended.

### What other drivers see

The report itself — type, place, and your note. **Not who reported it.** The app
never identifies a reporter to other users.

## Reporting a listing

If a listing is fake, has the wrong price, is already sold, is in the wrong
place, is offensive, or is a duplicate, report it. A person reviews every
report. We do not remove a listing automatically because it was reported, and we
do not tell the seller who reported it.

Do not use reports to attack a competitor. Repeatedly filing false reports is
itself a breach of these rules.

## Behaviour

Treat other people here as you would in person. No harassment, no threats, no
abuse, no publishing anyone's personal information.

## When we act

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A mistake | Nothing. Reports get dismissed; that is the system working. |
| A pattern of inaccurate reports | Your reports stop being published to others. |
| Deliberately false reports | Account suspended. |
| Harassment or threats | Account suspended, and referred to the authorities where the law requires. |

If you think we got it wrong, write to [support@…]. A person will look again.
