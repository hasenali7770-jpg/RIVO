# RIVO — AI photo and video enhancement (draft)

**Draft for legal review.** Shown to sellers at the enhancement step.

RIVO can improve how your listing photos look. This page says exactly what that
does, because a buyer travelling across Baghdad to see a house deserves to find
the house that was in the photos.

## The rule

**Enhancement may change how a photo looks. It may not change what the property
is.**

## What it may do

- Correct exposure — lift a dark room, recover a blown-out window.
- Correct white balance — remove the orange cast from indoor lighting.
- Sharpen a slightly soft image.
- Reduce noise in a photo taken in low light.
- Straighten a crooked horizon and correct lens distortion.
- Crop edges, without cutting out part of the property.
- Improve video stabilisation and pick a cover frame from your own footage.

## What it will never do

- Remove or hide anything — damage, cracks, damp, clutter, a pylon outside the
  window, a neighbouring building.
- Add anything that is not there — furniture, fittings, a garden, a swimming
  pool, a view.
- Replace the sky, the surroundings, or the time of day.
- Generate any part of an image that was not photographed.
- Change the apparent size, height or proportions of a room.
- Merge photographs of different properties.

These are not guidelines. The operations the enhancement service is permitted to
perform are on a fixed list in the code, and a request for anything on the
forbidden list raises an error rather than being carried out.

## Your original is always kept

Every enhanced photo is stored as a **separate copy**. Your original is never
overwritten, never deleted while the listing exists, and always available. You
choose which version appears on the listing, and you can switch back at any
time.

RIVO's reviewers see both versions side by side.

## If enhancement changes the property

The listing is rejected, and you will be told why. A listing photo that
misrepresents a property is not a presentation choice — it misleads someone into
a journey, and sometimes into a payment.

## If enhancement is unavailable

If the service cannot be reached, your original photos are published as they
are. Nothing is lost and nothing is delayed. Enhancement is optional; a listing
never needs it.
