## 1. What RIVO is

RIVO provides two things: navigation and traffic information (خرائط), and a
marketplace where people advertise property for sale or rent (داركم).

**RIVO is not a party to any property transaction.** We do not own, inspect,
value, or guarantee any property advertised here. We are not an estate agent and
we take no commission on a sale or a rent. The 3,000 IQD fee is for publishing a
listing, nothing else.

## 2. Your account

Sign-in is by your own phone number. Keep access to it; anyone with your phone
and its code can use your account. Tell us at [support@…] if you lose control of
it. One person, one account.

## 3. Listing a property

By publishing a listing you confirm that:

- you own the property or are authorised to advertise it;
- the details, the price and the map pin are accurate;
- **the photos are of this property, taken recently**, and are yours to publish;
- the property is genuinely available on the terms stated.

Full rules are in the [listing policy](./LISTING_POLICY.md).

Every listing is reviewed by RIVO before it appears. We may reject it, and we
will tell you why. We may remove a published listing that turns out to breach
these terms.

## 4. The listing fee

Publishing a listing costs **3,000 Iraqi dinars**, charged once per listing. It
is payable before review, and it pays for the review and for the listing's place
on the platform — **it does not buy approval.** A listing rejected at review is
not charged again when you correct it and resubmit.

The fee is not refundable once review has begun, except where Iraqi consumer law
requires otherwise.

## 5. Navigation — read this

**Routes, arrival times and road reports are guidance, not instructions.**
They come from map data, from other drivers' anonymous measurements, and from
reports submitted by the public. They can be wrong, out of date, or missing.

You are driving. Watch the road, obey the signs and the law, and use your own
judgement. Do not follow a route into a situation that is unsafe or unlawful
because the app suggested it. Do not operate the app while driving.

RIVO is not liable for a decision you make while driving.

## 6. Reporting road conditions

Report only what you have actually seen. Do not report a road closed to keep
other drivers away from it. Reports from an account that repeatedly submits
false ones stop being published. See the
[community policy](./COMMUNITY_POLICY.md).

## 7. What you may not do

- Advertise a property that does not exist, is not available, or is not yours.
- Use photos of a different property, or edited photos that misrepresent this
  one.
- Publish another person's contact details or personal information.
- Harass, threaten or abuse anyone.
- Scrape, copy in bulk, or resell RIVO's content or data.
- Attempt to reach parts of the system you are not authorised to reach.
- Use RIVO for anything unlawful under Iraqi law.

## 8. Your content

You keep ownership of your photos, videos and text. You grant RIVO the licence
it needs to store them, show them in the app, and display them in search
results and Reels, for as long as your listing is published. That licence ends
when you delete the listing.

## 9. Availability

can. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service.

## 10. Suspension

We may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms, that repeatedly
publishes false listings or false road reports, or that we reasonably believe is
being used fraudulently. Where we can, we will say why first.
