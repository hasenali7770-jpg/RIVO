# RIVO — Property listing policy (draft)

**Draft for legal review.** Shown to sellers before their first listing.

Every listing is reviewed by a person at RIVO before it appears. This page says
what we look for, so a rejection is never a surprise.

## What may be listed

Houses, apartments, shops, buildings, land and commercial property in Iraq, for
sale or for rent. One listing per property. If the same property is genuinely
offered both for sale and for rent, that is two listings.

## What every listing must have

| Requirement | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **8 to 18 photos** of the actual property | Fewer than eight cannot show a property honestly; more than eighteen buries the ones that matter. Enforced by the system — you cannot submit outside this range. |
| A map pin on the real location | Buyers navigate to it. A pin in the wrong district wastes everyone's time. |
| A truthful price in Iraqi dinars | For rent, say whether it is monthly or yearly. |
| An accurate area in square metres | |
| A description of this property | Not a general advertisement for your office. |

## Photos

- **Of this property, taken recently.** Not the neighbours', not a similar unit,
  not a photo from a website.
- **Yours to publish.** Do not use a photographer's or another agent's photos
  without permission.
- **No people's faces**, no car number plates, no documents with personal
  details visible.
- **No watermarks, phone numbers or logos burned into the image.** Contact
  details belong in the contact field.

You may use RIVO's photo enhancement. What it may and may not change is set out
in the [AI enhancement disclosure](./AI_DISCLOSURE.md); in short, it may improve
how the photo looks and may not change what the property is. Both the original
and the enhanced version are kept, and reviewers see both.

## Reels

Optional, one per listing, and it must be of that property. At least 1080p on
its short edge. No music you do not have the right to use.

## What gets a listing rejected

- The property does not exist, is already sold or rented, or is not available.
- The photos are of a different property, or are edited to misrepresent this one.
- The price is not the real asking price — including a low price posted to
  attract calls.
- The map pin is in the wrong place.
- Someone else's contact details appear in the listing.
- The description is an advertisement for a business rather than a description
  of a property.
- The listing is a duplicate of one you already have.

A rejection always comes with a reason. Correct the listing and resubmit it —
**you will not be charged again**; the 3,000 IQD fee is charged once per listing.

## After it is published

Keep it accurate. Mark it **sold** or **rented** when it goes. A listing left up
after the property is gone wastes buyers' time, and repeatedly doing it can cost
you the ability to publish.

Anyone can report a listing. Reports are reviewed by a person, not acted on
automatically.

## Repeated breaches

Occasional mistakes get a rejection and an explanation. An account that
repeatedly publishes false listings, fake prices or misleading photos will be
suspended.
